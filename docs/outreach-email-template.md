### 中文內容

> 您好，
>
> 我是「台灣家庭防災手冊」（https://disaster-handbook.vercel.app）的開發者。冒昧來信，想向貴組織介紹這個免費開源專案，並誠摯邀請您協助審校我們正在建置的多語版本。
>
> **這個網站在做什麼？**
>
> 使用者只要輸入地址，3 秒內就能找到家中最近的：
>
> - 避難收容所（6,300+ 筆）
> - 防空避難設施（74,000+ 筆）
> - 醫療院所含急診責任醫院（16,900+ 筆）
> - AED 位置（15,000+ 筆）
> - 消防隊、派出所、護理機構
>
> 系統會自動產生一份 PDF 家庭防災手冊，內容包含家人白天可能所在地、緊急聯絡人、集合地點，以及依住家類型客製化的避難建議。所有資料都在瀏覽器端處理，**不會上傳至任何伺服器**，關閉頁面即清除。
>
> **為什麼對移工特別重要？**
>
> 我們意識到，台灣約有 75 萬名移工朋友，其中很多人：
>
> - 住在雇主指定的地點，對周遭避難資訊不熟悉
> - 可能遇到語言障礙，無法快速理解中文防災訊息
> - 緊急時需要知道雇主聯繫方式、1955 勞工專線等資訊
> - 可能沒有家人在台灣，更需要預先建立的緊急聯絡計畫
>
> 這個工具希望能在地震、颱風、停電等突發狀況時，提供第一時間的關鍵資訊。
>
> **目前的進展**
>
> 網站目前已支援中文與英文。我們剛建置完越南語、印尼語、泰語、菲律賓語的基礎架構，但翻譯內容仍是初版（機器輔助翻譯），特別是防災用詞牽涉人命安全，我們非常希望能由母語人士協助審校，確保使用者能清楚理解。
>
> **想請教貴組織：**
>
> 1. 是否有機會協助審校其中一種或多種語言？可以透過 GitHub 提交、或我們另外提供試算表給您標註
> 2. 從貴組織接觸移工朋友的經驗，**有什麼功能或資訊是我們應該加入的嗎？**（例如：特定的求助專線、常用的緊急詞彙、移工社群實際會用到的應變流程）
> 3. 是否有希望加強或改進的地方？
>
> 如果您有興趣，我們很樂意安排線上或實體會議說明，也可以寄送網站的使用示範影片。若您無法直接協助，但能推薦其他合適的對象或志工，也非常感謝。
>

>
> 期待您的回覆，感謝您對移工朋友的長期付出。
>

### 英文內容（可附在中文之後）

> Dear [Organization Name] team,
>
> I'm writing to introduce a free, open-source project — **Taiwan Family Emergency Handbook** (https://disaster-handbook.vercel.app) — and to invite your help in reviewing the multilingual versions we're preparing for migrant workers.
>
> **What the site does:** Users enter an address and instantly find the nearest disaster shelters, air raid shelters, hospitals, AEDs, fire stations, and police stations. It then generates a personalized PDF family emergency handbook including family members' daytime locations, emergency contacts, meeting points, and custom evacuation tips. **All data stays in the user's browser — nothing is uploaded.**
>
> **Why it matters for migrant workers:** Taiwan hosts around 750,000 migrant workers, many of whom live at employer-assigned locations unfamiliar to them, face language barriers during emergencies, and don't have family nearby for rapid coordination.
>
> **Current state:** We support Chinese and English. We've just added the infrastructure for Vietnamese, Indonesian, Thai, and Filipino, but the translations are initial machine-assisted versions. Since disaster information is safety-critical, we strongly need native-speaker review before promoting these languages.
>
> **We'd love to ask:**
>
> 1. Could your team help review one or more of these languages? (via GitHub, shared spreadsheet, or however works best for you)
> 2. Based on your experience, **what features or information should we add** that would actually serve migrant workers better? (e.g., specific hotlines, common emergency phrases, evacuation workflows migrant communities actually use)
> 3. Any areas you'd like to see improved?
>
> We're happy to schedule a call or send a demo video. If direct collaboration isn't feasible, any referrals to native speakers or volunteers would also be greatly appreciated.
>
> This is a completely free, open-source, non-profit project built on Taiwan government open data. The code is public on GitHub.
>
> Thank you for your ongoing work supporting migrant workers in Taiwan.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]
> [Contact]
> https://disaster-handbook.vercel.app
> https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-disaster-handbook
